# Quick start

1. Open Fluent365 (on your phone: install it first, see ANDROID_INSTALLATION.md).
2. Answer 4 short questions: your name (optional), level, goal and daily time. Not sure of your level? Choose B1.
3. On the Home screen press **Start today's session**.
4. Follow along. Every screen has one big button at the bottom: **Check**, **Next** or **Continue**.
5. When you see **Well done!**, today is finished. Come back tomorrow.

## Tips

- Say words and sentences out loud. It helps a lot.
- When you get something wrong, read the explanation and type the correct answer once. That is how you learn it.
- Busy day? Under the Start button: *Do a 25-minute short day instead*.
- Missed a day? No problem. There's no "catching up". Tomorrow's plan just continues.
- Every week or two: Settings → Backup → Download backup.

## Words you'll see

- **Review**: words you've learned before come back just before you'd forget them.
- **Again / Hard / Good / Easy**: tell the app how well you remembered. It decides when to show the word again.
- **4 · 3 · 2**: tell the same short story three times, faster each time. It builds smooth speaking.
- **Active minutes**: time you were actually practising. Idle time isn't counted.
