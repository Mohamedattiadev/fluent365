# Put Fluent365 on your Android home screen

1. Open **Chrome** on your phone.
2. Go to your Fluent365 address (for example `https://yourname.github.io/fluent365/`).
3. Wait until the Fluent365 welcome screen appears.
4. Tap the **⋮** menu (three dots, top right).
5. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**). Tap **Install** again to confirm.
6. A Fluent365 icon appears on your home screen. Open it from there from now on.

Why install? It opens full-screen like an app, works without internet, and the phone keeps your progress safer.

## Sharing a Praktika lesson (or any PDF/text) to Fluent365

After installing:
1. In Praktika, download or open the lesson summary PDF.
2. Tap **Share** and choose **Fluent365**.
3. Fluent365 opens the Import Centre. Check the words and corrections, untick anything wrong, then tap **Add to my practice**.

If Fluent365 is not in the share list, open the PDF in your Files app and share from there, or use Import Centre → *Choose a PDF or text file*.

## Good to know

- Your progress is stored on this phone only. Download a backup now and then: Settings → Backup → Download backup.
- Samsung Internet and Firefox can also install web apps, but sharing files to Fluent365 works best in Chrome.
- On iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen. Sharing files into the app isn't supported by iOS.
