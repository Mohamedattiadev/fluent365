# Word lists

- **NGSL 1.2** (New General Service List): the 2,809 most useful words of general English.
  `NGSL_12_with_English_definitions.csv` was converted from the original .xlsx; `NGSL_12_stats.csv` gives the frequency ranking.
- **NAWL 1.2** (New Academic Word List): 963 words of academic English beyond the NGSL.

Both by Browne, C., Culligan, B. and Phillips, J., http://www.newgeneralservicelist.com,
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Downloaded 2026-11-05, unchanged apart from the
format conversion. The decks made from them (public/decks, with Turkish and Arabic meanings added)
are shared under the same licence.
